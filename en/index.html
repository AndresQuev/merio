Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Your transactions, assets and debts are stored only on your iPhone. We don’t send them to any server.

How do I back up my data?

Go to Settings → Export my data to get your transactions and net worth as CSV files you can save to Files, email to yourself or open in a spreadsheet. There’s no sync between devices yet, so keep a copy before switching iPhones.

I forgot my Net Worth passcode

On the passcode screen, tap Forgot your passcode? and confirm with Face ID or your iPhone passcode. You can then create a new one; your data isn’t deleted.

Why doesn’t a stock price match the exchange?

Stock prices are indicative: they come from tokenized versions that track the stock and trade around the clock, so they may differ slightly, especially while the market is closed. Stocks that aren’t on the list can be added with a manual value.

Where do prices and exchange rates come from?

Crypto and stock prices come from CoinGecko, and exchange rates from ExchangeRate-API (updated once a day). None of these requests include your data.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Delete account. This removes your account and all your data from this iPhone. It can’t be undone.

Do “Support the project” contributions unlock anything?

No. They’re voluntary contributions that keep the app free and ad-free. They’re handled by Apple; for a refund, follow Apple’s instructions at reportaproblem.apple.com.
