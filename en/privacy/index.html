Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Merio is an app for tracking personal spending, income and net worth, developed by Andrés Quevedo. This policy explains what data the app uses and why.

What stays on your iPhone

Your transactions, categories, assets, debts, value history, profile photo or avatar and settings are stored only on your iPhone. We don’t send them to any server and can’t see them.

Your Net Worth passcode is stored encrypted in the iPhone keychain. Face ID is handled by iOS: the app never receives or stores any data about your face.

What we do collect

DataWhenWhy
Email, name and account identifierWhen you sign in with Google or Apple (through Firebase Authentication, by Google)To identify you and keep your data separate from other accounts on the same iPhone
Feedback text, email (optional), app version, iOS version and iPhone modelOnly if you send feedback from the appTo read and reply to your feedback and reproduce issues. Feedback doesn’t store your account or name
Time of your last feedback, linked to your account identifierWhen you send feedbackTo limit submissions to one per minute and prevent abuse

We don’t use advertising, third-party analytics or cross-app tracking, and we don’t sell data.

Third-party services

  • Firebase (Google): sign-in and feedback storage. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Sign-In and Sign in with Apple, depending on the account you choose.
  • CoinGecko and ExchangeRate-API: the app downloads crypto and stock prices and exchange rates. These requests include no personal data.
  • Apple (App Store): handles voluntary contributions. We don’t receive your payment details.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept for as long as your account exists. Feedback is kept while it’s useful to improve the app; you can ask us to delete yours by writing to us.

Your rights

You can export your data (Settings → Export my data) and delete your account at any time (Settings → Delete account), which removes your account and all data stored on the iPhone. To access, correct or delete other data, write to merio.app@gmail.com.

Children

Merio is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date above and, for important changes, let you know in the app.
